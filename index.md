<p>As an artist I work with graphic design and my own manga; as a musician I record and release tracks. Those directions share a wider setting that keeps moving between text, drawings, and game development. The game is currently a prototype, while its world continues to grow in other formats.</p>

<p>As captain of Cult Of Maids, I care more about atmosphere, personal example, and enjoyment of the game than rigid structure. The team leans into unusual drafts, high tempo, and combinations built around the players rather than a fixed template.</p>

<p>My game experience also includes Minecraft server administration, from anarchic projects to fantasy modded servers. Some of those communities grew to several hundred players, which added community management to the technical side of the work.</p>

<p>The technical side includes Python and C++, with growing Java and C# experience. Alongside software, I also build and customize hardware solutions, combining code with practical engineering.</p>
