<section class="project-detail-section">
  <h2>About the project</h2>
  <p>Zombex:RE is an independent post-apocalyptic survival project currently kept at the prototype stage. The game is being developed in Unity while the wider setting continues to grow through drawings, writing, music, and other creative experiments.</p>
  <p>The project is not presented here as a finished release. This page documents its current state and gives the game a stable home that can be expanded as development produces more concrete material.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-detail-section">
  <h2>Connection to the wider universe</h2>
  <p>The same fictional setting also appears in the manga <em>Life and Sufferings of Kirik</em> and the light novel <em>Chronicles of Pavuk</em>. These works explore the world through different characters and formats rather than serving as simple copies of the game.</p>
</section>
